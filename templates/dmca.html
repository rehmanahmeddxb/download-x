DMCA Notice & Takedown Policy

YT Downloader X Pro is a personal, self-hosted download manager. We do not host, store, mirror, or redistribute any third-party content. All files downloaded through this software remain on the device of the user who initiated the download. As such, the project itself is not a “service provider” in the 17 U.S.C. § 512(c) sense, and we cannot remove files that exist only on individual users’ devices.

Filing a notice

If you are a copyright owner (or are authorized to act on behalf of one) and believe that content downloaded by a user of this software infringes your copyright, please send a written DMCA-compliant notice containing all of the following:

  1. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed (or a representative list if multiple works).
  2. Identification of the URL or other specific location of the allegedly infringing material, with enough detail for a user to locate it.
  3. Your contact information: full legal name, mailing address, telephone number, and email address.
  4. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that use of the material in the manner complained of is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  5. A statement, made under penalty of perjury, that the above information is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or are authorized to act on the owner’s behalf.
  6. Your physical or electronic signature.
Where to send it

Send completed notices to our designated DMCA agent:

  • DMCA Agent
  • YT Downloader X Pro
  • Email: dmca@downloadsx.app
  • Subject line: DMCA Notice

We aim to acknowledge valid notices within 5 business days. Please do not send general support inquiries to this address.

Counter-notice

If you believe content you downloaded was wrongly identified as infringing, you may submit a counter-notice that includes: your physical/electronic signature, identification of the material and the location where it was removed, a statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good-faith belief the material was removed or disabled as a result of mistake or misidentification, your name/address/telephone, and a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the federal district court for your address (or the jurisdiction of the service provider if outside the US) and that you will accept service of process from the complaining party. Send counter-notices to the same email above.

Repeat infringers

Because this software is self-hosted, we have no central account system. Operators of public deployments are encouraged to keep their own abuse logs and to act on valid DMCA notices in line with the safe-harbor provisions of 17 U.S.C. § 512.
